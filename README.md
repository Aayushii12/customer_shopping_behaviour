## Project Overview
This project is an end-to-end Data Analysis project built using Python, SQL, and Power BI.  
The objective of this project is to analyze a retail company’s consumer behavior dataset (3,900 transactions) to uncover insights into spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior, ultimately guiding strategic business decisions to improve sales and long-term loyalty.

The project follows the complete Data Analytics workflow:
- Data Preparation & Feature Engineering
- Database Integration & SQL Analysis
- Data Visualization & Dashboard Development
- Business Reporting & Recommendations

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains **3,900 transactional records** with 18 key features, including:
- **Customer Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status
- **Purchase Details:** Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color
- **Shopping Behavior:** Discount Applied, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type, Payment Method

---

##  Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Transformation (Python)
- **Missing Data Handling:** Imputed missing `Review Rating` values using the median rating specific to each product category for higher accuracy.
- **Data Standardization:** Renamed all columns to `snake_case` for consistency and better SQL compatibility.
- **Feature Engineering:** 
  - Created an `age_group` column by binning customer ages into quantiles (Young Adult, Adult, Middle-aged, Senior).
  - Created a `purchase_frequency_days` column by mapping categorical frequency text (e.g., "Fortnightly", "Annually") to numeric day values.
- **Redundancy Check:** Verified that `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` were perfectly correlated and dropped the redundant column to optimize the dataset.
- **Database Integration:** Loaded the cleaned DataFrame directly into a relational database using `SQLAlchemy`.

### 2. SQL Analysis (PostgreSQL / MySQL)
Performed 10 advanced analytical queries (utilizing CTEs and Window Functions) to answer key business questions:
- Compared total revenue generated by different genders and age groups.
- Identified high-spending customers who still utilize discounts.
- Ranked the top 5 products with the highest average review ratings.
- Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.
- Analyzed the revenue and average spend difference between subscribers and non-subscribers.
- Segmented customers into **New**, **Returning**, and **Loyal** based on previous purchase counts.
- Identified the top 3 most purchased products within each category using `ROW_NUMBER()`.

### 3. Power BI Dashboard
Created an interactive, stakeholder-ready dashboard containing:
- **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Average Order Value, Total Customers, and Subscription Rate.
- **Visuals:** Revenue breakdown by age group, customer segmentation pie charts, and shipping type vs. spend bar charts.
- **Interactivity:** Dynamic slicers for Category, Season, and Location to allow drill-down exploration.

##  Key Business Insights & Recommendations

- ** Boost Subscriptions:** Subscribed customers demonstrate a higher average spend and total revenue. Promoting exclusive subscriber benefits (e.g., free express shipping) can increase Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
- ** Reward Loyalty:** Customers classified as "Loyal" (10+ previous purchases) drive a disproportionate amount of total revenue. Marketing budgets should shift toward retaining and rewarding this segment.
- ** Optimize Discount Policies:** While discounts drive volume, a significant portion of high-spending customers still use promo codes. Implementing targeted, personalized discounts rather than blanket offers can protect profit margins.
- ** Leverage Shipping Preferences:** Customers opting for "Express" or "Next Day Air" shipping have a notably higher average purchase amount, indicating they are prime targets for premium product campaigns.
- ** Targeted Demographic Marketing:** The "Middle-aged" and "Senior" demographics show the highest total revenue contribution and should be the primary focus for high-ticket item promotions.
